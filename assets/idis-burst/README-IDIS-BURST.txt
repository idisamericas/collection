IDIS APPLICATION / PRODUCT BURST
================================

Build 48 supports up to eight lightweight static application/product images:

shot-01.webp
shot-02.webp
shot-03.webp
shot-04.webp
shot-05.webp
shot-06.webp
shot-07.webp
shot-08.webp

Recommended: 900-1200 px wide WebP, quality 80-88. Transparent WebP is fine.
The files are lazy-loaded only after the IDIS side starts. Missing files are skipped.
At 10 seconds, the blue/teal blur and plus field appear, then available shots begin flying out from the center through a preserve-3D field.

Use product/application imagery such as:
- schools
- banking
- stadiums
- retail / grocery
- casino / hospitality
- government
- cameras / NVRs
- IDIS Center / VMS application screens

Static WebP is recommended instead of another video because it avoids decoding a second simultaneous video stream and usually uses far less bandwidth.
