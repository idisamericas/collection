IDIS IMAGE BURST — BUILD 50

The IDIS interactive scene now uses two independent image folders.

FIRST HALF OF IDIS VIDEO
assets/idis-burst/set-1/shot-01.webp through shot-08.webp

SECOND HALF OF IDIS VIDEO
assets/idis-burst/set-2/shot-01.webp through shot-08.webp

The switch is driven by the actual IDIS showcase video currentTime/duration.
The burst starts with the blue/teal environment at 10 seconds. If the video is already past halfway when the burst begins, set-2 is used immediately.

Recommended assets: 900–1200 px wide WebP, quality 80–88. Keep similar aspect ratios for the cleanest 3D field.
